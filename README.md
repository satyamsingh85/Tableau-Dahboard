# Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Tableau dashboard designed to track and analyze Amazon sales performance across different product categories, sub-categories, geographical locations, and time dimensions.

## 📊 Project Overview

This dashboard provides a comprehensive look at retail e-commerce performance. It allows stakeholders to quickly break down performance metrics to identify top-performing product segments, seasonal trends, and regional sales distribution.

---

## 🛠️ Key Features

* **Category Analysis**: A bar chart highlighting total sales across major product categories (Technology, Office Supplies, and Furniture).
* **Sub-Category Breakdown**: Deep-dive sales performance view for individual sub-categories (Chairs, Storage, Tables, etc.).
* **Geographical Sales Map**: An interactive map visual showcasing US state-wise sales distribution to identify regional market penetration.
* **Monthly Sales Trends**: A multi-year line graph comparison (2021 vs. 2022 vs. 2023) to analyze seasonality and year-over-year revenue growth.

---

## 💻 Tech Stack & Tools

* **Data Visualization**: Tableau Public / Tableau Desktop
* **Data Sources**: Amazon / E-commerce Sample Superstore Dataset
* **Key Visual Elements**: Bar Charts, Geographic Maps, Multi-series Line Charts.

## 📈 Key Insights from Visuals

* **Top Driver**: The **Technology** category leads overall sales generation, followed by Office Supplies.
* **Sub-Category Champion**: **Chairs** stand out as the highest revenue-generating sub-category within the dataset.
* **Growth Patterns**: The dual-axis trend analysis shows high sales volatility across months, with notable recurring peaks at specific times of the year, indicating potential holiday or promotional shopping spikes.
